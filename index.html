A little memory app

Keep what words can’t hold.

Keepsake saves your memories by the sense that holds them. The smell of clean sheets, the crackle of a record, the taste of too-sour lemonade. Say it or type it, and it joins a reel that never stops turning.

How it works

Three little steps.

No accounts to set up and no blank page to stare at. Kip asks, you answer.

1

Pick a sense

Smells like, sounds like, tastes like. Each sense has its own colour, so your memories sort themselves.

2

Say it, or type it

Speak freely and the words are written down for you to edit. Add up to three pictures if you like.

3

Spin the reel

Every memory lands on a wheel of tiny polaroids. Drag it, flick it, tap one to open it.

The shaker keychain

Shake a memory loose.

Tiny polaroids and stars rattle around inside a keychain charm. In the app you shake your phone, drag the charm or tap the button, and one of your memories falls out. Try it here.

Sound

Memories with their own soundtrack.

Keepsake listens for clues in your words and plays what it hears: a blanket rustling, leaves, birds, rain. Tap one to hear it.

Sounds are made live in your browser. Turn your volume up a little.

Seven senses

What does it … like?

Every memory starts with one of seven little sentences. Tap one to read it.

Meet Kip

A snail who carries your memories.

Kip says hello when you open the app, asks how you are and why you’re here, and walks you through it. Along the way he leaves a dotted trail, the same dashes you’ll find on his antenna. Give him a tap.

Hi! I’m Kip. I carry little memories on my back, the ones words can’t hold.

Come and keep something.

Open Keepsake on your phone, say hello to Kip, and save the first thing that comes to mind.
